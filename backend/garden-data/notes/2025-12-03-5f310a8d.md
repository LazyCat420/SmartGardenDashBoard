# Garden Note - 2025-12-03

**Lettuce (Buttercrunch Butterhead):** Tight heads forming in positions A1-A3, diameter 5-6". Leaves crisp, no tip burn. Estimate harvest-ready in ~5 days. Second succession planting (Red Oak Leaf) at 2-week stage.

**Cilantro (Santo):** Bolting prematurely in 74°F temps—pulled mature plants showing seed stalks. Reseeded Santo variety in shadier corner with better airflow. Added slow-bolt variety (Calypso) to trial.

**Jalapeños (Early & TAM Mild):** Healthy dark green foliage, no pest pressure. Early variety showing 4-6 developing peppers at 2-3" length. TAM Mild slower to set but flowers abundant.

**Mint (Spearmint & Chocolate Mint):** Spearmint sending aggressive runners into adjacent containers—trimmed back 40% of growth. Chocolate Mint more contained but still vigorous. Considering separate isolation containers for both.

**Strawberries (Albion Everbearing):** Second flush of blooms post-June harvest. Hand-pollinating daily with soft brush—10-12 flowers open currently. Early fruit development on 6 berries from first flush.
